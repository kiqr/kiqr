# KIQR design

The look of the KIQR web UI, the CLI's wording, kiqr.dev and kiqr.cloud. The tokens live in `web/src/tokens.css`; this file says why they are what they are and how to use them.

KIQR is modelled on ContentWriter (`~/Projects/contentwriter`, `apps/web`): same dark palette, same sidebar, same card language, same live crawl screen. KIQR does everything ContentWriter does and more (evidence for every fact, Seemo, posts as well as articles), so when a screen exists in both, copy ContentWriter's layout and add KIQR's extras below or beside it rather than redesigning it.

## The idea

A deep navy workspace. Panels sit one step lighter with hairline borders; items inside panels one step lighter again. Teal is the one action and live-state colour, indigo the second accent for anything the AI is doing, lime only for Seemo's mark. The crawl screen is where the product shows off: a live radial map of the site, a progress ring, an activity feed and the business profile assembling itself.

## Color

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--bg` | #070a12 | page ground |
| `--panel` | #0c1220 | cards (at 80%), sidebar (at 60%), inputs, menus |
| `--panel-2` | #111a2c | items inside cards, chips, hover |
| `--line` / `--line-2` | #1c2740 / #2a3754 | hairlines / input and outlined-button borders |
| `--fg` / `--muted` / `--dim` | #e6ecf7 / #8392ad / #56627c | text / secondary text and card labels / meta and placeholders |
| `--accent` | #5eead4 | teal: primary buttons (dark text on it), links, active nav, live state |
| `--accent-2` | #818cf8 | indigo: AI and learning (the learning panel, empty-state icons, gradients with teal) |
| `--signal` | #d7ff43 | lime: Seemo's mark and launcher. Nothing else. |
| `--ok` `--warn` `--bad` `--skip` | greens, ambers, reds, slate | status pills, graph nodes and dots |

**The business's own colours.** On the business profile the site's first two brand colours (collected from its pages and stylesheets) become `--brand` and `--brand-2`, and only identity decorations use them: the hero glow, card icon tiles, brand-term tokens, avatars, the voice quote and card hover edges. Actions stay teal everywhere. Without brand colours the profile falls back to indigo and teal.

Gradients only teal→indigo, and only for emphasis: the onboarding headline, the progress ring, confidence bars. Topic clusters use ContentWriter's cluster palette (teal, indigo, pink, amber, green, blue, orange, violet).

## Type

- Inter throughout: page titles 600 20–24px, item names 500 14px, body 14px/1.55, descriptions 12px muted.
- Card labels: 11px, uppercase, 0.08em tracking, muted (`.cap`). Nav group labels the same at 10px, dim.
- JetBrains Mono for paths, URLs, counts and timings.

## Shape and depth

Radii: 8px buttons, inputs, nav items; 12px items and the project switcher; 16px cards. Almost no shadows; edges come from hairlines. Floating things (menus, Seemo panel) get `--sh-lg`.

## Layout

Sidebar 256px, as ContentWriter: wordmark; the project switcher (globe tile, "Project" label, name, chevrons; menu lists projects and "Add project"); grouped navigation with lucide icons and a teal highlight that slides between items, grouped by what the user is doing — To do on its own at the top, with a teal count of open approvals, drafts and failed publishes (the page is "Getting started" and carries the step-by-step guide until every step is done or skipped); Marketing (Plan, Performance, Calendar); Library (All, then one item per asset type with its count, shown only once the project has assets of that type; nothing is pinned); Business (Business profile, Competitors, Content with its Site map and Content gaps sections; crawls have no item of their own, the profile and Content pages carry a Rescan button and the crawl screen opens from there); Seemo (Chats, Tasks); Setup (Abilities, Channels, Project settings; Abilities is the one page for everything Seemo can do here: rows under Yours, From the marketplace and Built in with the state, what each includes and a switch for this project, "Teach Seemo something", the marketplace below, Advanced at the end; every row and card opens a modal); footer with Settings and where data lives. Settings (for the whole installation) has its own sub-navigation: General (status tiles, this installation), Models (an Abilities block first: Make images, Make video, Make music, Send email, each with the connection behind it or "off: add a connection"; then provider/model per task, with whether it can run here), Crawling (defaults), Storage (what is and isn't in history, backups). Edits collect in a floating save bar; saving writes config.yaml and commits. What each project publishes to lives under the project's Setup → Channels. Content area up to 1360px, 28/40px padding. Below 900px the sidebar stacks on top. On first run (no projects) there is no sidebar: onboarding stands alone, centred on a faint grid.

Business profile, as ContentWriter, opened by a brand hero so the user sees their own business first: the site's share image as a slowly drifting cover, its icon (or initial) on a glowing tile, the business name in the site's own primary font, its tagline, language, regions and social links, with Export and Rescan website in glass buttons; then animated counts (products & services, audiences, pages read, facts with a source, topic clusters, content gaps; zero counts are hidden); then the version line with an Edit button that turns every card editable (save makes a new version); cards, each with a lucide icon tile: What the business does | Brand kit (logo, colour swatches that copy on click, font specimens), Products & services (product/service icons) | Audiences (initial avatars in the brand colours), Tone of voice (the site's own example sentences rotating in a large quote, then traits, examples, avoid), From your website (a gallery of the site's share and hero images), Brand terms | Regions served, then KIQR's additions (What sets it apart | Competitors, Pricing), Existing topic clusters, Content gaps we found, and at the bottom every fact with its source page and quote, by section, with edit and lock.

## Motion

Motion shows state and is otherwise still.

- Crawl screen: ported from ContentWriter. The radial site map grows as pages are found, with a sweeping beam and packets along edges; nodes colour by status; the progress ring and stats tween; the activity feed slides rows in with a teal wash; discoveries pop in; the phase timeline fills Discover → Crawl → Learn → Profile; the learning panel streams page reads, then profile sections.
- Onboarding: headline, text and field rise in one after another; submitting lifts the form away and the crawl screen rises in while the sidebar slides in.
- Route changes: content rises in over 360ms. Cards on the profile rise in, staggered. The active nav highlight springs between items.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` turns all of it off.

## Components

- **Buttons** (`.btn`): 8px radius. `.p` teal primary (one per screen), `.g` outlined secondary, `.t` text. Labels name the action: "Rescan website", "Save new version", "Build the profile".
- **Chips** (`.chip`): status and counts; `.ok` `.warn` `.bad` `.acc`, `.ai` (indigo, scheduled or made by Seemo).
- **Library** (`library.css`): type strip, then a grid of asset cards — 16:9 thumbnail (the asset's first image, otherwise its type icon on a wash of the brand's accent, indigo/teal until the brand is known), type tag on the thumbnail in the all-types view, title, summary, status chip, where it was published, who made it (person or Seemo) and when. An asset page previews by the type's `preview` (Markdown rendered, images, video, audio, campaign YAML), with status, origin, publications and files beside it; unknown types fall back to the file list. A post is shown as it will look in the feed (`PostCard`): the brand as the author (icon or initial on the brand's accent, site name, handle), the network as a quiet tag, the text with links and hashtags in the accent, the image full width below, a muted action row and the character count against the network's limit, all on a dark stage. In the library grid a plain click on a post opens that card in a modal over the grid (status chips, publish, "Open the page"; Esc or the backdrop closes it); modifier clicks and other types go to the page.
- **Cards** (`.pcard`), **items** (`.it`: name, uppercase kind tag, description), **tokens** (`.tk`: brand terms, traits; italic block tokens for quoted sentences).
- **Abilities** (`settings.css`, `.ab-*` and `.mk-*`): the options strip (a picker per contract with two or more ready candidates, shown only then); rows (icon tile green when on, name, state chip, vendor, included parts as small pills, the project switch) under Yours, From the marketplace and Built in; "Teach Seemo something" is a form (what Seemo should be able to do, when, instructions with three starter shapes, this project or every project); the marketplace is a search field, shelves, vendor chips and a grid of cards (icon, name, vendor, version, cost, summary, pills for what it includes, the gradient Install button, Details). The modal (760px, portal, Esc and backdrop close, `#ability=<id>` in the URL): header with icon, name, vendor, version, state chip; tabs Overview (summary, when Seemo uses it, What's included as a list with details, where it came from), Instructions (file tabs for a core ability, the Markdown, Edit a copy for this project / for every project, drop a copy, back to the shipped text) and Connect (keys for this project or every project, preferred ability per contract); footer with the two switches, Update, Remove. **Channels**: rows with a teal mail or send tile, the destination in mono, the connection, the count; "New channel" picks a kind and fills its destination fields. The asset page gets a Publish box (publications so far, a channel to publish to).
- **Chat** (`chat.css`): the Chats page is a 260px list (teal "New chat", title, time, last line, a pulsing teal dot while Seemo answers) beside the conversation in one panel; the widget shows the same conversation compact on every other screen. Your messages are teal bubbles on the right; Seemo's are plain text beside its lime mark, with the tools it used as a folded indigo line ("3 steps") and, while it works, one live indigo line ("Reading the brief…"). Cards sit in the flow: an approval (indigo edge, Approve and run / Decline, the schedule in words and the next three runs, the allowed actions) becomes a task card (status chip, live step, Open in Tasks) once approved; settings changes list key, old and new in mono; an ability card (Seemo wants to learn something) shows the name, when it applies and the text; a publish or email card shows the title, where it goes, the connection, the first lines and the attached files, and becomes "Published" or "Sent" with the link. An image a step produced shows under the step line, up to 360px wide, and opens full size. Notes from KIQR (a run finished) are small centred pills. The composer is one growing textarea with a teal send button that turns into Stop while Seemo answers. Tasks are cards with an indigo icon tile (teal while running), schedule, next and last run, allowed actions and a fold of runs; the calendar is ContentWriter's agenda (weeks, a date column, items) with repeating tasks beside it.
- **Plan** (`profile.css`, `.plan-*`): cap "Marketing", h1 "Plan", one teal button ("Ask Seemo for a plan", or "Ask Seemo to review it"). Then the period at a glance (`.plan-hero`, a card with a faint teal and indigo wash): the period as a teal chip with the version in mono, the summary at 15px, the channels in play as tokens with a send icon (their network names), and on the right a teal→indigo progress ring with the measured objectives' average progress ("62% towards the targets"; a done objective counts as 100%, "not measured yet" when nothing is). Objectives as a grid of cards (`.plan-goal`): title with the change against the previous window as a mono teal or red arrow and percentage, the why in muted 12.5px, a 7px teal→indigo bar towards the target (striped when nothing measures it), then the KPI label in mono and "**now** of target" on the right (linking to Performance), and a foot with the date, the campaign count and, when unmeasured, "Not measured yet" in amber. Campaigns as cards (title, status chip, Done and Remove as text buttons, goal; for an active campaign with dates a thin timeline from start to end with today as a dot; objective, channels, who proposed it, then the tasks and assets that belong to it). Empty state: a target in a circle, "No plan yet". In the chat the plan card lists the objectives with their KPI and target and "Approve the plan"; a campaign card shows the goal and, for a change, the from/to pairs.
- **Performance** (`profile.css`, `.perf-*`): cap "Your business", h1 "Performance", an outlined Refresh; "Plan KPIs" as tiles (KPI label with a target icon, the objective, the 30-day value with a trend arrow in green or red, "of target · 63%" and a thin teal bar; dimmed with "Needs a traffic source" when nothing measures it); then one section per source with tiles (metric, last 7 days with the trend against the 7 before, the 30-day total, a sparkline, teal when the shape is good, amber when not) and tables for the top queries, pages, events and channels; last the sources list with a green dot when connected, "installed but not connected" in amber with a Connect link, or a marketplace link. Empty state: a bar chart in a circle, "No source connected yet".
- **Asset actions** (`chat.css`, `.aa`): one block, by status, under a post in the chat (`.chat-embed .foot`, with the status chip and "Open in the library"), in the Library modal and page, and in To do rows. Draft: "Approve for {channel}" (teal primary) or a channel picker; no channel: a line with the link to add one. Approved: chip "Approved for {channel}", Publish now (primary), Schedule (outlined, opens a datetime row), Change channel, Archive (text). Scheduled: indigo chip with the time and channel, Publish now, Unschedule. Published: the publications list, Unpublish (text, with its tooltip). Archived: "Bring it back". A failed scheduled publish is a red line above. Status chips read Draft, Approved, Scheduled, Published, Archived; nobody picks a status.
- **To do** (`.todo-*`): cap "For you", h1 "Getting started" with the step-by-step guide (`.lib-guide`) at the top until its steps are done or skipped, then h1 "To do"; the count chip; sections "Waiting for your yes" (the same approval cards as the chat, each with "In the chat …"), "Needs a hand" (failed publishes), "Drafts to approve" (the post cards with their actions), "Approved, not sent yet", "Coming up" (rows: icon, title, time, chip, actions). Empty state: a check in a circle, "Nothing waiting".
- **Field** (`.field`): the onboarding URL input with the `https://` prefix and the action inside.

## Writing

The interface speaks to the user in the second person, plainly, sentence case (the uppercase card labels are styling, written in sentence case in code). Seemo speaks in the first person as a colleague: specific, calm, no exclamation marks, no emoji. Screens that are not built yet say so plainly ("Coming in the next release") rather than showing fake content. UnblockingAPI is mentioned only where pages could not be read.
